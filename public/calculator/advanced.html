How the math works (same model as the 2025–2029 Broker Compensation sheet): New-to-Medicare enrollments pay the full CMS initial FMV in the enrollment month. Plan transfers pay a prorated amount — (annual renewal ÷ 12) × months remaining in the calendar year. After a member's first plan year they join the renewal book and pay (annual renewal ÷ 12) per month; the book erodes monthly by your erosion assumption, and each year's new sales join the book at your first-year retention rate. FMV figures are CMS maximums — actual carrier pay varies by plan and contract, and some plans pay $0. This is a planning projection, not a guarantee of income.
