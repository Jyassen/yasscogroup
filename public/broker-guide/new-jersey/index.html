The path — New Jersey

Five steps to Ready-to-Sell

The default path is the Life, Accident and Health license — 40 hours of pre-licensing, two state exams, fingerprints, then the application.

  1. Pre-licensing course — 40 hours

    20 hours Life + 20 hours Accident & Health from a New Jersey-approved provider. Course from about $169.95 (self-study, e.g. ExamFX). Your completion certificate never expires — bring it to the exam.

    Approved provider list: NJDOBI Licensing & Education (item 20) · direct list (Excel)

  2. PSI state exams — two separate tests

    New Jersey has no combined Life & Health exam. Each is multiple-choice, 70% to pass. Accident & Health: 88 questions, 210 minutes. Fee: $38 per exam ($76 for both). Test center or remote proctored. Score reports are valid 1 year.

    Schedule: test-takers.psiexams.com/njins or (855) 579-4634 · Candidate Bulletin

  3. Fingerprints — before you apply

    Live Scan through IdentoGO, $66.05. Your application cannot be processed until results arrive. Service code 2F16Y3.

    Enroll: uenroll.identogo.com or 1-877-503-5981

  4. Apply through NIPR — $170 + $5.60 fee

    You must be 18+, a New Jersey resident or with your principal place of business in NJ. Exam results are submitted automatically.

    Apply: NIPR — New Jersey resident licensing

  5. Stay licensed — renew every 2 years

    Renewal lands on the last day of your birth month: 24 CE credits including 3 ethics, renewal fee $150. Passing candidates may qualify for a 60-day temporary work authority under a licensed producer's supervision.

    Licensing help: inslic@dobi.nj.gov · (609) 292-7272

After you are licensed

Certify, contract, and start writing

AHIP Medicare + Fraud, Waste & Abuse training every year — about $175 (50 questions, 2 hours, 90% to pass, 3 attempts). Then carrier product certifications, appointments, Ready-to-Sell, and E&O insurance.
